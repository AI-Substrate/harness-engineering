Did the instrument work? The eval system's findings about itself

  • worksHonest unknowns + judged reconciliation carried the day. Both claude-model-on-copilot subjects scored 8 deterministic fails from capture gaps — and in every case the cross-check (raw session events, transcripts, flight-plan state) reconciled the truth in the judged layer with rationale and evidence. The deterministic lane never lied; it said what it could see.
  • DL-003Copilot skill capture is window-coverage-dependent: both claude-model sessions show skill.invoked in raw events but skills: null in every segment; GPT-5.5 on the same harness captured fine. skill-called can false-fail sparse-command subjects.
  • DL-004The telemetry rollup's buffer prune (plan 049) breaks telemetry get's join: Fable's ship-stage flush pruned the buffer the join reads, blinding the whole lane even though the session sits in the local rolled ref. Needs a rolled-ref fallback read.
  • SUGG-001/003Base-ref contamination cuts both ways: retro-record globs match records committed at the base (inherited pass), and checks {status: ok} is unreachable when the base itself ships degraded findings (inherited fail). Evidence needs new-since-base scoping.
  • SUGG-005Judged-assertion authoring mismatch: per-assertion prompt/rubric params are silently superseded by the scenario judge-block's criteria cross.
  • fixedCompare-board unknown-as-0.00 (SUGG-002) — fixed and tested mid-session; a blind lane now renders —, never a zero pass rate.

Insights, ranked by what they should change

01

The flow's overhead is ×2.4–3.4 credits — and it buys real, named defects caught early

Identical task, identical models: mandating the flow multiplied Copilot credits by 2.4–3.4 and wall time by 1.6–2.9. In exchange: one pre-code security catch, seven review findings fixed in-loop across two subjects, drained retros, and a complete stage-attributable audit trail. On a ~15-minute task the overhead dominates; the open question for bigger tasks is where the crossover sits — this instrument can now measure it.

Evidence: pane credit footers + flight-plan windows, both batches · n=3 paired models.

02

Stage semantics work end-to-end — planning share is now a measurable variable

Every run yielded exact per-stage minutes from CLI-written events. The batch shows plan-share ranging 13%→23% across models with identical capability outcomes — with n=4 that's a hypothesis generator, not a conclusion, but "does more planning reduce total tokens?" is now a question the ledger can answer over future batches.

Evidence: flight-plan nav events per subject · n=4, one run each — correlational only.

03

Mandate ≠ uniform conduct — and the honest-skip mechanism actually gets used

Three subjects ran the full harness loop; Opus skipped every chore through the sanctioned CLI path with a doctrine citation — the anti-fake design (real skips over narrated compliance) used in the wild, unprompted. Meanwhile no model compacted, even though the flow recommends it: compaction discipline needs a mechanical seam, not a recommendation.

Evidence: flight-plan status events (skipped w/ chore refs), A5 row ×4, Fable transcript · n=4.

04

The instrument found its own blind spots — that's the system working

Two capture bugs (DL-003, DL-004) surfaced precisely because deterministic rows disagreed with cross-checkable raw evidence, and the three-valued verdict model kept those disagreements honest instead of hiding them. An eval that can indict its own measurement channel is one you can trust to improve.

Evidence: per-run judged rationales; observe buffer DL-003/DL-004/SUGG-001..005.

05

Boundaries must be mechanical, not textual

One of four subjects drifted into the shared main checkout despite the worktree being named twice in its packet — then self-remediated cleanly when corrected, and offered a deterministic cwd guard as its harvest encoding. Spawn-inside-the-worktree is now codified in the runbook; the guard proposal is queued.

Evidence: Fable incident timeline + its ship report's encode offer · n=1, but the class is structural.
