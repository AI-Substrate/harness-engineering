## Scope
How to tell whether the harness is actually helping: the handful of measures worth tracking, what each one tells you, and how to gather it without turning measurement into productivity theatre. The guiding rule (from the foundations): **measure facts separately from interpretation**, and measure *ease of entry, safety of change, speed of proof, and compounding* — not activity volume.
