And so we meet the first, and most important, of them all. The boot extension. Every harness loop, without exception, begins here. Its task is to ask one simple question: is this repository ready for work? In every codebase the answer looks a little different. But at its core, it checks that the code still builds, that the services it needs can wake and run, and a good deal more besides. Modest, at first. But it'll grow, in time, into something far more capable. For now, though, this single extension is all we need.
